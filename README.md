### `🌱 about me`

I build tools that **I actually use** — and when they get too good to keep to myself, I publish them.

I like **AI inside real products**: not a loose chatbot, but AI with a clear job — a coach that decides your training load, a narrator that tells the story, a commentator that explains a Bible chapter in plain language.

House rules:

- 🧪 **test before shipping** (almost everything live has a test suite)
- 📱 **it has to work on mobile** — that's where life happens
- 🆓 **free to use**, always
